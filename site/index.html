Scheduled Payments

Sign a payment once. A TapeOut circuit decides when it unlocks. Our server delivers it — and anyone can deliver it if we go down.

How It Works

1
Schedule
Set receiver, amount, start time, and how often to pay. Funds are locked in the contract.
2
Circuit Gates
A TapeOut on-chain circuit (built from NAND gates) checks whether conditions are met.
3
Auto-Deliver
Our server watches every block and executes payments the moment the circuit outputs 1.
4
Or Anyone Can
If the server goes down, anyone can submit the payment from the Due page and earn the tip.

Why Prokairos

Non-Custodial
Funds sit in the smart contract. Only the receiver, the tip, or a refund to you can ever leave.
Circuit-Gated
TapeOut hardware-style logic on-chain. No oracle, no off-chain data — pure deterministic gates.
Decentralized Fallback
Anyone can execute a due payment. The tip creates an open execution market.
Stablecoin Native
Pay in USDC or USDT on X Layer. Fast, cheap, and denominated in dollars.
